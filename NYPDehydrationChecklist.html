A common goal is around 64 oz per day, but needs vary by person, activity, and weather.

What to do now

  • 1Drink small sips of water or an oral rehydration drink.
  • 2Rest in a cool place and avoid heavy activity.
  • 3Try fluids with electrolytes if you have been sweating, vomiting, or had diarrhea.

Get urgent help now if

  • !Confusion, fainting, or trouble staying awake
  • !No urine for many hours or very little urine
  • !Severe vomiting, severe diarrhea, or inability to keep fluids down
  • !Chest pain, severe weakness, or signs of shock
